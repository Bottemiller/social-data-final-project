The Global Pulse

Melbourne: the city of… hmm, not sure. As exchange students from the United States, our team was curious about this Australian city. After discovering that our exchange friend, Tracy, was from Melbourne, we couldn't help but want to dig into the "pulse" of the city. Is it more like Copenhagen or Houston, Texas?

To answer this, we analyzed the 2025 hourly trends of pedestrians using the City of Melbourne open data, generated from over 60 sensors placed strategically throughout the Central Business District. By exploring these patterns, we can begin to understand the "why" behind the way this city moves.

Visualizing the Trimodal Weekday Pattern

Figure 1. This graph compares the average pedestrian counts during the week and on the weekend. Notice the trimodal weekday pattern representing the morning, lunch, and evening rush, while weekends show a smoother curve driven more by leisure and nightlife activity.

The Friday Bridge

While Monday through Thursday follow predictable work-centered patterns, Friday acts as a bridge into the weekend. Evening pedestrian activity becomes more spread out and nightlife activity increases significantly compared to the rest of the week.

Weekly Pedestrian Heatmap

Figure 2. This heatmap compares pedestrian activity across every day of the week and every hour of the day. Friday evenings show much stronger activity than other weekdays, helping reveal Melbourne’s transition from work-focused movement to weekend social behavior.

A City in Motion

The animated sensor map reveals how the city changes throughout the day. Morning activity clusters around transit and office areas, while afternoon and evening movement shifts toward shopping districts, markets, and nightlife locations.

Animated Pedestrian Sensor Map

Figure 3. This animated map visualizes the changing “pulse” of Melbourne throughout the day. Bubble size represents pedestrian volume at each sensor location, allowing readers to observe how activity shifts spatially and temporally.

The Verdict

Ultimately, our research revealed a city constantly shifting between work and leisure. Melbourne’s weekdays reflect the structured rhythm of a global financial center, while weekends reveal a more relaxed, social, and walkable culture. This flexibility is one of Melbourne’s greatest strengths and helps explain why the city feels so dynamic throughout the week.
